Each section reflects a different part of my creative identity — forged by hand, crafted in code.

## 💍 Jewelry Design

My jewelry work explores form, symbolism, and the fine art of wearable sculpture.  
I work primarily with silver and gold, incorporating motifs like serpents, talismans, and elemental designs.

**Skills & Focus:**
- Hand fabricatio
- Stone setting
- CAD modeling for jewelry
- Mythic and symbolic design themes

**Sample Projects:**
- **Serpent Coil Ring** – A coiled silver ring inspired by ancient protection symbols  
- **Dragon’s Eye Pendant** – A cabochon set in layered metal scales  
- **Runeband** – A custom wedding band inscribed with Nordic runes

---

## 🔧 Welding

My welding work is distinct from jewelry — larger scale, more structural, often industrial or sculptural.  
Where jewelry is intimate, welding is elemental — raw material shaped by fire.

**Techniques:**
- TIG and MIG welding
- Micro welding for small-scale precision
- Metal finishing and polishing
- Custom repairs and functional metalwork

**Applications:**
- Artistic sculpture  
- Furniture joints & decor  
- Structural repairs  
- Creative fabrication for mixed-media art
